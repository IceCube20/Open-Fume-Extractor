BEFORE POWER-UP

Commissioning checks

  1. Verify the selected module family and display resolution.
  2. Use a stable USB cable and keep the OFE RS485 bus disconnected while flashing.
  3. After installation, open the serial console at 115200 baud and confirm a clean boot.
  4. Reconnect the bus only after the target starts with the expected firmware name and version.
